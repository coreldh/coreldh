# Rodrigo Díaz Jonguitud

I run several software projects with AI agents, on a system I built so that someone without a
coding background can ship real work. I am not an engineer. I work in Level 2 technical support.

## How I got here

I started working seriously with AI less than a year ago. Before that I used it the way most
people do: research for videos, images, small things. In March 2026 I set my other creative
projects aside and went all in on using it for serious work.

I did not come from software. I studied a little HTML and CSS years ago, nothing more. What I
have instead is a system: it records every decision, it keeps several projects moving in
parallel, and nothing goes out into the world without my sign off.

## What I shipped

I use [Lavish](https://github.com/kunchenguid/lavish-axi), Kun Chen's editor for HTML artifacts,
almost every day. You open a page in it, click any part of it, and leave a note there for your AI.

The notes were text only. An image is worth more than a thousand words, so I asked for image
attachments. Then I built it, and Kun reviewed it and merged it into his own project.

- The request: [issue #103](https://github.com/kunchenguid/lavish-axi/issues/103), opened 2026-06-24
- The work: [pull request #188](https://github.com/kunchenguid/lavish-axi/pull/188)
- Merged on 2026-08-14 by the maintainer, [@kunchenguid](https://github.com/kunchenguid)
